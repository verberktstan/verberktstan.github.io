Swark

Swark collects the small functions I kept rewriting in every project. They're not trivial enough to inline, and not big enough to justify a heavier dependency.

(swark/key-by :id [{:id 1} {:id 2}])        ;; => {1 {:id 1} 2 {:id 2}}
(swark/map-vals count {:a [:b :c] :d [:e]}) ;; => {:a 2 :d 1}
(swark/jab / 10 0)                          ;; => nil

Highlights include:

  • memoir, a memoize with selective cache flushing;
  • a minimal map validator, where the spec is a map of predicates;
  • swark.authom, which attaches hashed auth tokens to maps as metadata.

It has been maintained since 2024 across 50+ releases. Most of it is portable .cljc, it has a kaocha test suite, and it's deployed to Clojars with deps-deploy.
